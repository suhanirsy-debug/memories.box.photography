Welcome to Memories Box Photography

Where moments turn into timeless memories.

I'm Deepak Kumar Gupta, a professional photographer based in Kolkata, West Bengal. With a passion for storytelling through images, I've been capturing life's precious moments for 38 years.

From the sparkle of a wedding day to the joy of welcoming a new baby, and from the glow of maternity to the energy of a concert, I aim to freeze those moments in a way that's authentic and beautiful. My lens captures it all — the love, the laughter, the drama, and the excitement.

"Our approach is simple: create a relaxed, fun atmosphere, and let the moments unfold naturally. 📸"

Global Reach 🌍

With experience working across the globe including India, Singapore, Malaysia, Dubai, Thailand, Vietnam, and Nepal. We are ready to capture your story wherever you are.
